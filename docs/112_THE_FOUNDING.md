# 112 — The founding: a table of elements in the core, matter by bonding and release

This document scopes a founding in which nothing is placed. The bounce
derives a table of elements by weight, and the whole table stands inside the
universal core. Everything outside the core emerges later, as the bonds
inside it tighten and its pressure releases matter through the throat.

**Status:** F1, F2, F3 and F5 built (§8); F4 and F6 open. It replaces the cascade's shells
(`pulse::cascade`) and the galaxies standing at the founding
(`pulse::deploy`). It realises docs/111 §5 and depends on docs/104 §6 (the
band) and docs/111 §4 (the collections).

## 1. What stays: the table

The founding's one input is the bounce's nucleon count, in grains (for
example 2^40). Bounce nucleosynthesis (`pulse::founding`, docs/97 §8–§14)
turns it into the table `T` of nuclides by weight:

- the seal window;
- the neutron share at its fixed point;
- the seeds;
- the climb along the nucleus's convergence;
- the free neutrons returned as hydrogen.

Nucleons are conserved exactly, and the result is read beside nature, never
fitted to it. The count only sets how much matter the table describes.

## 2. The state at the founding

| Holder | Holds at the founding |
|---|---|
| The universal core's throat (`Body.throat` of the home) | the whole table `T`, unreleased |
| The firmament (the home's flows the throat holds wholly) | nothing |
| The zero galaxy (the home's flows outside the throat) | nothing |
| The ocean, other galaxies | nothing |

The universal core's state is the table's total holding: all the grains
together hold one of its rings (`universal_state`), so the throat radius is
1 in the shared unit (docs/111 §2). The unit of mass is the home's
(`deploy` already takes this case when no galaxy stands).

## 3. The cycle of a bounce

Each bounce runs four stages in order. Each stage is an existing law, applied
to matter that is now inside the core.

### 3.1 Release from the throat into the core

The release law (`breath`, docs/96 step 3) releases from each kind the
throat holds the share

    s = p·c/(c + base),     c = p·w̄/w

on the inverse unlocked face (docs/104 §1, the push row), in whole nuclei.
Released matter enters the home's flows inside the throat as motes, at the
throat's phase for this bell. This is where unbound matter first becomes
moving matter. Heavier kinds have smaller `c` and leave the throat more
slowly.

### 3.2 Bonding tightens: the collective

The motes inside the throat are the firmament's collective. Each bounce
perturbs it (docs/111 §4):
- motes the bounce moves together, on one hemisphere and with open frames,
  bond;
- a collection moves as one, with one spin and one displacement.

**Tightening.** A collection is held as one body. In the band (docs/104 §6)
it is carried by its combined weight `W = Σ w` over one formula unit of its
members, not by any single member's. Its rest point is

    R*·R* = w̄_c / W

So bonding pulls matter inward: a bonded collection rests deeper than any of
its members would alone.

### 3.3 Pressure builds

The collective's pressure is the substrate's (docs/106 §1; docs/110 §3):

    P = hydrostatic + thermal + magnetic

- **Thermal:** the torque's undelivered share becomes heat (agitation). Each
  contained bounce adds to it.
- **Magnetic:** the open frames of the unbonded motes.
- **Hydrostatic:** the column the deeper matter bears.

Bonding lowers the magnetic term, because paired frames are holding.
Agitation raises the thermal term.

### 3.4 Release through the throat: matter outside the core

Matter leaves the core where its pressure share outweighs its potential
share at the throat, by the restoring law (E9) applied at the throat
surface, `R = H`:

    s_throat = P_in/(P_in + P_out) − Φ_in/(Φ_in + Φ_out) > 0

Equivalently, its band rest point lies beyond the throat (`R* > 1`, so
`W < w̄_c`). A collection that heat parts (its members no longer moved in
step) gives up its bond. Its lighter pieces have smaller `W`, rest beyond
the throat, and cross out radially (`flows::exits`, `R ≥ H`) into the zero
galaxy.

**The emergence.** Bonding holds matter in and pressure lets it out. What
crosses is light, unbonded or newly parted matter, lightest first. Each
crossing raises `w̄_c`, so the next kinds follow. The transmission
`τ = left/founded` opens with the first crossing (docs/111 §4.5). Until then
the echo is contained.

## 4. After the release

Everything outside the core is matter that crossed the throat:

1. **The zero galaxy** is the released matter about the universal core. It
   receives the front only once `τ > 0` (docs/111 §5.3).
2. **Bodies** form from its gas by the collapse law (`flows`, "collapse where
   the motes are") and are read by docs/111 §3:
   - a collection that passes its pinch is self-held;
   - one whose heat crosses the ignition law is a star;
   - its extent, density and throat are readings.
3. **Galaxies** form where released matter holds its own reach about the
   universal core: the pinch test, read live (docs/111 §5.4). A galaxy's core
   then runs the same cycle (§3) on its own held matter, one tier down.

## 6. Build order

- **F1. The founding holds the table in the throat.** `deploy` puts `T` in
  the home's throat. There are no shells, no beads and no galaxies. The
  firmament and the zero galaxy start empty.
  Witness: the founding conserves nucleons; every kind of `T` is in the
  throat; no other core stands.
- **F2. The release on the inverse face.** The release law reads `s =
  p·c/(c + base)` and releases into the core's flows inside the throat.
  Witness: nucleons conserved per bounce, heavier kinds released more
  slowly.
- **F3. The band as an attractor** (the item still open from docs/104 §6).
  Each bell a mote's spin moves toward its rest value
  `ℓ* = R*·H(<R*)` by the attractor share, with what it gives or takes
  booked to the core's reservoir. Witness: a mote displaced from `R*`
  converges and does not oscillate; `Σ m·ℓ + reservoir` is exact.
- **F4. Collections carried by their combined weight** (§3.2). Witness: a
  bonded pair rests deeper than either member alone.
- **F5. Release through the throat by the pressure share** (§3.4), and `τ`
  as a running book of what the founded table has lost through the throat.
  Witness: `held + left = founded`; with the band's push removed nothing
  crosses.
- **F6. The contained period** `T_f` and the front's containment (docs/111
  W4).
- **Read.** A bounded read, bounce by bounce:
  - what the throat, the collective and the zero galaxy hold;
  - the bell of the first crossing and its composition;
  - `w̄_c`, `τ`, and the first body to form;
  - conservation and the torque identity each bounce.

## 7. Open

1. **The rim.** With no shells at the founding, the S^360 rim needs its own
   reading. The enclosure's rim law, `rim = H` (`Enclosed::whole`), puts it
   at 2 in the core's own units. `T_u` and the bell's length in core rings
   (`orbit_galaxies`' `t_u/4`) must be re-read on it.
2. **A collection's weight.** Is `W` the sum over one formula unit of its
   members (a molecule), or its holding per grain? §3.2 takes the former;
   the two give different rest points.
3. **Heat parting bonds.** §3.4 relies on agitation breaking the in-step
   motion. Whether the existing heat law does this, or a bond needs its own
   strength against heat (the nuclear grip `κ·h`, `nuclides::grip`), is a
   read.
4. **Xylarium.** Its founding stands galaxies at `t = 0` and has every galaxy
   hear the core from the start. Under this document both change; that is a
   decision for its operator.

## 8. Built

- **F1.** `pulse::deploy` stands the home alone, its throat the whole table
  and its flows empty; `Universe::founded` is the table's holding. The bell's
  period is still read from the cascade's echo (§7.1).
- **F2.** The release law's reach `1 + span·c` is read through the throat
  (`flows::through`, `R → 2 − R`). A kind carried past the centre stands at
  the magnitude on the far side, its phases at the antipode.
- **F3.** `flows::toward_rest`: the band's step, halved where a whole step
  would carry the mote past its rest (`R·R = 2q`, compared on the squares).
- **F5.** `τ = (founded − throat − firmament)/founded`, the firmament by held
  share (`pulse::firmament_holding`).
- Witnesses:
  - `the_founding_holds_the_whole_table_in_the_universal_cores_throat`;
  - `the_band_as_an_attractor_converges_on_its_rest_without_crossing_it`.
