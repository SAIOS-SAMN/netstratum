## 1. LAW-COUPLING v2 (#241; 78.182 item 9, 78.181)

### 1.1 The dipole

A body of `p` protons and `n` neutrons, `w = p + n`, holds `3p` and `3n`
dimensions, both read from the body (`chem::Dimensions`: `open = 3Q`,
`held = 3Z`). The electrons' unpaired ends `U` stand beside the dipole.

- **The bound share** at the body's place, on the passage face, `δ = H/(H +
  R)` with `H` the hold enclosed within its reach: in netstratum's
  orientation the held share is `g = 1 − δ`, so the unlocked share is `u =
  δ`.
- **The push,** the protons' charge left open by their bound electrons, and
  its antipole, the held stratum:

      π = p·u/w = p·δ/w        π̄ = (n + p·g)/w = 1 − π

- **The pole** in the push coordinate is held minus open, `σ = π̄ − π = 1 −
  2π`. A bare nucleus pushes with its whole proton share; a neutral body
  does not push.
- **The strength** is the bond ends, `U/3w`, never a pole. It replaces v1's
  fluidity `o` (the open share of the dimensions) in the coupling's grip.
- **The vacuum face** applies `I` to the face-readings: `π ↦ 1 − π`, the
  pole reversed. The strength names no face and is unchanged (docs/104 §0).
- **A bond** binds electrons and moves share from `π` into `π̄` through `g`;
  `3w` and both counts are kept.

### 1.2 Spread bodies: each isoclinic plane its own

Each isoclinic plane `p`, the dual pair `(e_k, e_kR)` of S³ (docs/128 §10.0),
carries:

- **its hold share** `W_p`, first degree, the body's holding split by its
  spin on the pair: `W_p = 3W·(|ℓ_k| + |ℓ_kR|)/Σ_j(|ℓ_j| + |ℓ_jR|)`, so
  `Σ W_p = 3W` (an even `W` each where the body does not turn);
- **its reach** `r_p`, the pair's own circle, `ℓ_k/H(<ℓ_k)` (docs/128 §1);
- **its chiral bivector pair**, `(ℓ_k, ℓ_kR)`, read on the halves as `ℓ±_k`
  (docs/128 §9.3).

The exchange runs on each plane's own chart. The transfer across planes is
weighted by `α_p = W_p/3W` (`Σ α_p = 1`), never by an L2 norm across
planes. The magnet on reach (docs/121 §14) reads each plane at `r_p` with
its own extent and the push and strength above; the body's lift moves by
`Σ_p α_p·m_p`.

is refused by name, `UnbodiedDipole { pole_plane }`, replacing v1's
