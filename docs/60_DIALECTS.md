# 60 — Dialects: senses, speech, and the shared tongue

Netstratum adopts all proposals from the convergence-engine
review (its thinking, perception, entity and alphabet modules; mycelium's
seventeen-letter Cayley alphabet). Substantive work first; test only what
moves the cadence.

## The decisions

1. **No third alphabet.** Netstratum's minds do not get a custom fixed
   alphabet. The shared alphabet is Mycelium's seventeen immutable Cayley
   letters (AUTIC); Netstratum's dialects live in which words a line mints,
   how it perceives, and what it can hear, over one alphabet.
2. **Dialects begin in perception.** The register already names four
   perceptions (`PERCEPTIONS`: planar, volumetric, harmonic, umbral); until
   now only volumetric changed anything. Each planar-family perception is
   now a sense profile over four channels a word can use:

   | channel | ops | meaning |
   |---|---|---|
   | Sight  | the D4 plane words (rotations, flips, transpose) | shape and orientation |
   | Hue    | `Recolor` | colour identity |
   | Rhythm | `Tile` | repetition |
   | Touch  | `Object` (segment, keep, crop) | bodies |

   | perception | perceives | is deaf to |
   |---|---|---|
   | planar   | Sight, Hue, Touch    | Rhythm |
   | harmonic | Sight, Hue, Rhythm   | Touch |
   | umbral   | Sight, Touch, Rhythm | Hue |
   | volumetric | its own seat (unchanged) | — |

   **A sense bounds derivation, not use.** A mind cannot derive a word in a
   channel it does not perceive (its own think withholds), but a word it
   learned (licensed, heard, inherited) is used whole. Knowledge across a
   perception gap is therefore worth something: it can only be bought or
   inherited, never re-derived.
3. **Speech between lines.** Each line hears its nearest living neighbour
   on the torus (one word per stride). A word wholly inside the hearer's
   senses is heard: it joins the line's heard library and is licensed into
   its own when the line reconstructs it on its own task (the cores'
   inheritance by reconstruction, reused). A word using a channel the hearer
   lacks is misheard: counted, never adopted. Misheard counts per pair are
   the measured dialect distance.
4. **Translation loss is price.** The knowledge market prices a word at its
   depth plus one per channel the buyer cannot perceive.
5. **Hardness-weighted shared tongue.** Consilience already weights by
   hardness (unlike lines). Kept; the dialect distance is its measure of
   unlikeness once wired (D5).
6. **Words compete.** A library is bounded by use: a word unused for long
   gives way (D6). The chronicle keeps every word; only the working
   library forgets.
7. **Layered understanding.** value (hue) → object (bodies) → cell, the
   outer constraining the inner, scored on genuine pairwise data — never a
   difference of node values (convergence-engine's cocycle is identically
   zero by construction; do not repeat it) (D7).
8. **Words carry how they were found** (D8) and the cores carry open
   questions beside settled words (D9).
9. **The house remembers.** The seat saves its genome after each closure
   and restores it at start (D0).

## Measured at landing (D1-D4, 2026-10-08, beat 8220-8240)

Per speech stride (4 beats): 23-33 words heard, 18-28 misheard, across 65
lines (planar 27, harmonic 19, umbral 10, volumetric 9). About 45% of the
words a neighbour speaks cross a perception gap.

## Measured after D5-D10a (beat 8320-8392)

Solves per beat fell from about 4.7 (beats 6270-6720, before D1) to about
2.75: each planar-family line now withholds what its deaf channel would
have derived (D1). Learned words still close tasks whole; the market and
speech are now the only way across a perception gap. Resident memory flat
at 1.62-1.66 GB over six minutes.
