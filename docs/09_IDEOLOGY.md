# IDEOLOGY — the dual cohesion ledger (design stage)

**Status:** LANDED 2026-07-27, whole — the five-landing board (TRADE-1 →
ABSORB-1 → BOND-1 → COVENANT-1 → IDEO-1, `BUILD_ORDER.md` §3) shipped
every mechanic here with its protocol sections and witnesses (NS-1 §2.3
tags 30–38, 41). All rates, windows, weights, and curves remain
**placeholder parameters** that tune at balance time; the recorded
deferrals and their reopen paths live at `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q4.

**The founding insight (operator relay, 2026-07-27):** love, compassion,
morality, and integrity are tradeable structure, not flavour. Just as
`T_eq⁴` governs the physical thermodynamics of a sector, the cohesion
ledger governs its *social* thermodynamics: every environment, resource
constraint, and contact boundary forces a civilization to develop a
distinct ideological framework to survive. The design law of `06` extends:
there is no universal home, and there is no universal virtue — only the
alignment between a civilization's survival strategy and its regime. The
engine judges nothing and measures everything.

**The form ruling (same relay, recorded):** the "unitary morphodynamics"
formulation — encoding the four as continuous unitary field dynamics — is
REFUSED (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q3): continuous field transforms on floating
hardware diverge across architectures (the no-float law, CHARTER §3.2, and
SEAL B), and no deterministic transition function for "love" as a field
exists without inventing subjective physics. The lawful form is this
document's: four **deterministic behavioural ledgers over receipted
deeds**.

## 1. The measurement law (the founding ruling)

Ideology is **measured, never declared and never scored**:

1. **Deeds, not dials.** Every axis is a pair of cumulative exact-rational
   counters — a syntropic book and an entropic book, the VENT-1 sheet shape
   — incremented ONLY by receipted events. A sector cannot set its
   compassion; it can only act, and be read.
2. **Derived, never stored.** The four ratios derive at read on the
   observer surface, exactly as the syntropy ratio does (the VENT-1
   uplink precedent). The chronicle carries deeds; arithmetic over deeds
   is free and replayable.
3. **No oracle, no single number.** No rule ever reads a "goodness"
   scalar. Consequences attach through specific inscribed mechanics (§3),
   each citing exactly one axis. The four axes never collapse into one
   figure — that would be the scalar/scored form the ancestry law refuses
   (CHARTER §5), returning through the back door.
4. **Absence is zero, not sin.** Apathy — declining to act — is not an
   event and mints no receipt. It reads as an empty syntropic book, never
   as a phantom entry in the entropic one.

```
            THE DUAL COHESION LEDGER (per orb — eight counters)

        axis          syntropic book (earned)      entropic book (accrued)
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   compassion         given_in_crisis        ◄──►  drawn_in_crisis
   love (resonance)   bond_flow              ◄──►  severance_load
   integrity          covenants_fulfilled    ◄──►  covenants_breached
   morality           returned_across        ◄──►  extracted_across

          every entry grounds in a receipt · every ratio derives at read
```

Each axis reads as `syntropic / (syntropic + entropic)` — an exact
rational in `[0, 1]`, derived, with the empty-books reading a placeholder
parameter per mechanic (§3.1's ruling: the empty book is not a
credential). All posts are valued in joule-commensurable units (05 §1.4
made sink commensurable; other resources post through a placeholder
commensuration table, weight 1 in v1).

**Scope from birth:** the books are per-orb so every mechanism lands and
witnesses single-world — the P1 archipelago is society enough (a mother
and daughter colony are already distinct books). Civilization-scoped
reading is composition over a faction's orbs, and cross-empire reading
arrives with the worlds that meet at N5/N6; the machinery is identical,
only the counterparties multiply.

## 2. The four axes

### 2.1 Compassion — the absorption axis

The willingness to absorb a neighbour's crisis at one's own cost.

**Posting rule (deterministic):** a deed posts to the compassion books iff
the counterparty stands in a *receipted, uncleared crisis* at the deed's
stamp — famine-struck (tag 5, joule famine and its freeze-lock included)
without relief (tag 6), or overheat-struck (tag 22) without clear (tag
23). The chronicle orders the strike before the deed; classification is a
replay fact, never a judgment call.

- **`given_in_crisis`** (the giver's syntropic book): value transferred TO
  a struck orb (TRADE-1), or a struck orb's overflow absorbed into one's
  own banked sink (ABSORB-1) — taking on heat so the neighbour's array
  stays coherent.
- **`drawn_in_crisis`** (the taker's entropic book): value drawn FROM a
  struck partner — distress extraction — or heat dumped onto a struck
  absorber.

**In-game function:** prevents cascade failure across shared
infrastructure. A transfer that covers the one-tick upkeep bill relieves
at the landed relief law (02 §2.4, ECON-J) — compassion is the famine
relief mechanic made inter-orb, not a new physics.

### 2.2 Love — the resonance axis (bonds)

A persistent, non-extractive resource loop between two orbs: flow without
per-tick clearing and without a demanded reciprocal rate.

**The bond** is a receipted standing order — source, target, resource,
rate per tick. Flow accrues continuously as workspace against the
source's banked store and **settles openly by one receipt at touch**,
anchored on `last_touch` — the demurrage shape (tag 29), landed. Banked
state never mutates silently; if the source cannot cover at settlement,
the bond breaks as a receipted default (and a covenant breach, if
covenanted).

- **`bond_flow`** (the source's syntropic book): cumulative value streamed
  over live bonds. A symmetric pair of bonds is the marriage form; a
  single bond is patronage; neither is privileged.
- **`severance_load`** (the severer's entropic book): the standing rate ×
  the unserved notice window (placeholder) when a bond is cut without the
  covenanted notice. An orderly severance — notice honoured, or bond run
  to term — posts **nothing**: leaving is not a sin; dropping a partner
  mid-winter is.

**In-game function:** the physics already rewards constancy — bonded flow
moves at the tick and never sits accrued, so it ducks the leak (05 §1.2)
and demurrage (SINK-1) that batch shipping suffers. Long-horizon love is
cheaper than churn *by the landed decay laws*, not by decree. Severance
is a real shock: the target's next heating bill (ECON-J) arrives with the
inflow gone.

### 2.3 Integrity — the fidelity axis

Coherence between word and deed, measured at the escrow.

The carrier is the Glasskeeper escrow machinery (01 §7): covenants with
exact-rational conditions over chronicle data, collateral locked in the
vitrified matrix, release on fulfilment, `BREACH_AND_SLASH` on breach.

- **`covenants_fulfilled`** += the collateral-at-stake value on release.
- **`covenants_breached`** += the collateral-at-stake value on slash.

**Value-weighted, never count-weighted (inscribed ruling):** a thousand
toy promises must not wash one giant breach — the wash-trading exploit is
closed at design time by weighting both books in stake value.

**In-game function:** the fidelity ratio drives escrow collateral
requirements (§3.1). High-fidelity sectors post minimal collateral and
establish macro-routes with low verification overhead; a decayed ratio
prices distrust explicitly.

### 2.4 Morality — the reciprocity axis

The measured boundary policy: does a sector close its loops with the
outside, or treat external manifolds as uncompensated sinks?

Every cross-orb draw — energy taken, sink consumed, heat dumped into a
partner's headroom — opens a reciprocity entry with a settlement window
of `W` ticks (placeholder). Compensation flowing back within the window
closes the loop.

- **`returned_across`** (syntropic): value returned against draws inside
  the window — closed loops, the reciprocal boundary.
- **`extracted_across`** (entropic): the unclosed remainder at window
  expiry, plus uncompensated entropy dumps — the extractive boundary.

**Declared vs measured (the composition rule):** a sector's *declared*
policy is a covenant (§2.3's axis); its *measured* policy is this ratio.
A sector that declares reciprocity and measures extraction needs no
"liar" flag — its covenant breaches post to the fidelity books. The axes
compose without a judge.

### 2.5 The cohesion surface (observer)

`state.json` carries the eight committed counters per orb and derives the
four ratios at read — derived, never stored, the syntropy-ratio precedent
exactly. Presentation is fun-first (CHARTER §2): the player reads words
and exact fractions — "honours 5 of 6 covenants (5/6) · gave 15/4 joules
in crisis" — never matrix algebra. Fractions render whole (the landed
CLI discipline: "3/2", never 1.5).

## 3. What the ledger does (mechanical consequences)

### 3.1 Collateral scales with fidelity

Escrow collateral requirement is a placeholder curve on the fidelity
ratio — exact-rational, monotone, with an inscribed **ceiling**: a
zero-fidelity sector pays the maximum collateral, it is never barred
(01 §5 guardrail 6 — the lawful, costlier path always remains; ostracism
is never absolute). The empty-book reading is a parameter; the candidate
default is full collateral until a history exists — trust is earned, and
the empty book is not a credential.

### 3.2 Ideological friction (`ΔS_ideo`)

A transaction between two orbs whose measured reciprocity ratios diverge
generates additional entropy:

```
    ΔS_ideo = φ × |reciprocity_A − reciprocity_B| × volume        (φ placeholder)
```

posted through the landed VENT-1 machinery — accrues as workspace, seals
by receipt at collect, nothing un-receipted. The physical reading:
divergent boundary policies genuinely cost handling and verification
overhead. **Friction prices divergence, not vice:** two extractive
empires trade with each other frictionless. The engine keeps its promise
to judge nothing.

### 3.3 Covenant templates — exportable morality

Named, reusable covenant state machines a faction may adopt at founding
or export to satellites and partners (the "morality model" made a
shippable artifact):

- **the Compassionate Covenant** — a fixed fraction of each collect's
  surplus routes to a reserve pool for crisis-struck neighbours;
- **the Reciprocity Covenant** — every draw must close within the window
  or auto-slash;
- **the Bond Covenant** — severance requires the notice period, else
  slash.

All placeholder terms; each is ordinary COVENANT-1 machinery, no new
physics.

### 3.4 What is refused here (inscribed)

1. **The stored score** — any mutable "alignment stat" (the ancestry
   refusal; §1 rules the lawful form).
2. **The single goodness number** — axes never aggregate.
3. **Tokenized reputation** — no "trust certificate" ERC-1155: the
   fidelity ratio is a live chronicle reading, and a token snapshot forks
   the truth (SEAL B). NS-7 may anchor covenant *outcomes* (01 §7.3) —
   never the ratio itself.
4. **Count-weighted fidelity** — closed at §2.3 (wash trading).
5. **Phantom sin** — inaction posts nothing (§1.4).
6. **Purchasable virtue** — no book is incremented by payment alone;
   ideology attaches to deeds under the no-pay-to-win guardrail
   (01 §5 guardrail 1).

## 5. The closed-manifold note (world-book)

The world is an internal substrate inside the exterior universal
shielding: its books close *inside* the boundary — encased + vented =
excess, absorbed = sink consumed (05 §1.2, §1.4), composing at mesh scale
into the universe audit (OPEN_QUESTIONS Q2.3) — and only anchors cross it
(NS-7, the notary). Inside a closed manifold nothing "disappears":
unmanaged friction, predation, and breach degrade the shared interior
that every occupant lives in. The four axes are not sentiment — they are
the structural preservation strategies a civilization runs so its
internal manifold does not dissolve into thermal chaos where it touches
the outside. The world's physics explains its own ethics.
