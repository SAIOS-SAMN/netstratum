That is exactly the circularity `12_UNION` §3 and `18` §2c′ bar — and it

## §4 Why the reduction scopes, and why ∂Δ could not have

The operator ruled that coboundary reduction is the scoping of a
dimension, not `∂Δ`. The sealed code already performs exactly that
scoping, in two phases, and both are scoping acts
(`engine/rfa-core/src/reduce.rs`):

1. **Extent** — `Δ'_ij,l = Δ_ij,l − Δ_0j,l + Δ_0i,l`. Anchoring at
   vertex 0 sends every frame-induced relation to exactly zero; "the
   surviving entries are the frame-free content." This fixes what the
   dimension is: gauge vanishes, structure remains.
2. **Scale** — integer normalization by `lcm(denominators) /
   gcd(numerators)`, after which "every coordinate becomes an integer
   and the representative is scale-canonical." This fixes how far it
   extends. The scale is derived, never chosen.

Extent and scale together are the scope of a dimension, and the
reduction produces both without consulting any potential. This is why
D4's floor is written `ext_x( Π_G(Δ(V)) )` — the reduction is what says
how far capacity may fall, because the reduction is what knows which
extent was frame and which was structure.

**Why `∂Δ` could not have done this job — the hole the strike closes.**
A potential is chosen. Had `∂Δ` scoped the dimensions, structure would
have become an artifact of that choice, and a dimension carrying little
potential could be scoped out of existence silently, with no receipt.
That is precisely the discard the 2026-08-01 correction was fighting,
re-entering through the back door. Under the standing law a dimension
exists because the algebra found it, not because a potential rewarded
it — and D4 makes that enforceable: capacity holding genuine difference
(`orbit_lock ≠ ∅`) never contracts at all.

## §5 Why C cannot leave the mathematics — support for its survival as semantics

The foundation keeps C "only as the semantics of the component readings"
(§3 of the ledger: congruence over `(i,j)`, coherence over `(i,j,k)`,
cohesion over `(l)`). That survival is not a courtesy; it is forced, and
the reason is worth stating once:

`H¹ = Z¹/B¹` is defined only on cocycles. Where the coherence reading
over `(i,j,k)` is non-zero, `Δ ∉ Z¹`, so `Δ` has no cohomology class —
and D4's floor `ext_x(Π_G(Δ(V)))` is then reading a reduction of
something that has no well-defined class to reduce to.

**This section's original claim is struck (operator ruling 2026-08-01).**
It said the coherence reading is "the well-formedness condition that
makes the capacity floor meaningful" — that it gates. **That is still
giving `C` a pseudo-function, and it was a regression.** A gatekeeper is
an agent; `C` has no agency of any kind.

> `C` has zero operational function. It isn't a gauge, it isn't a score,
> and it doesn't get to act as a gatekeeper.

**What actually holds the ground — three primitives, no `C` anywhere:**

1. **Exact algebraic reduction.** `Π_G(Δ(V))` runs on `BigRational`
   linear systems and is total — it does not test its input, it projects
   it. The only question it answers is structural: is a relation a
   coboundary, or a non-trivial cocycle defect in `H¹`? That is a
   classification the reduction outputs, never an evaluation it performs.
2. **Witness-carried proofs.** Validity is binary and deterministic: a
   transition carries a valid O(step) `OrbitWitness` or it does not
   (`verify_orbit_witness`, landed `835d03b`). No scalar is consulted to
   decide.
3. **The unpartitioned extent read.** `ext_x` measures before and after
   reduction. A measurement, never a metric to optimize or descend.

None of the three computes `C`, and no engine path does. My §5 argument
— that `H¹ = Z¹/B¹` needs cocycles, so something must check — was about
textbook cohomology as a quotient. **The engine never forms that
quotient.** It reduces to a canonical representative and reads its
extent, and `coboundary_reduce` is total on any `Delta`. The gate I
insisted on is required by no operation, and §11 had already said the
right thing: non-composition roots, it is not refused. §5 then
re-introduced the refusal one section later — the contradiction was
inside this document.

What survives of §5: `C = Σ|R|` is an L1 collapse of a per-residual
distinction, it discards which triple broke and by how much, and it is
therefore a lens reading and nothing else. That is all it ever was.

**The predicate and the collapse are different objects — this resolves
the apparent conflict with Article I and §2b.** `24_FORM` §2b bars
collapsing a directional quantity to a scalar aggregate wherever the
system computes, and §2a above calls `C = x` a substandard character.
Both are right, and neither touches what §5 needs, because:

- **the cocycle condition** is a predicate, evaluated per residual and
  exactly: *is `Δ_ik − Δ_ij − Δ_jk` zero for every triple?* It
  aggregates nothing, collapses nothing, and has no direction to lose.
  `rfa_core::for_each_residual` already computes exactly this and
  already skips zeros — the sealed iterator is the predicate, not the
  sum.
- **`C = Σ|R|`** is that predicate's L1 collapse into one number. It
  discards which triple broke and by how much, which is precisely the
  information a lens wants and a judge must not lose.

The predicate is lawful, necessary, and already landed. The collapse is
the substandard character: barred where the system computes, permitted
where a reading is reported to an operator. **`coherence_functional` is the
collapse; `for_each_residual` is the predicate.** Wherever this document
or the ledger says "the coherence reading gates," the predicate is meant
— never the sum.

This is the kernel's own philosophy applied to the one place a score had
crept in: the kernel admits or refuses, it never optimizes.

### 8a. `k` growth — no deed is owed. `k` has no independent extent.

The relayed answer was that a receipted deed is strictly required for
compositional growth, lest capacity drift through a back door. **The
premise is false, and the sealed iterator says so:**

```rust
let n = delta.dim;
for i in 0..n { for j in 0..n { ... for k in 0..n { ... for l in 0..delta.fiber {
```

`i`, `j` and `k` are all bounded by `delta.dim`; only `l` is bounded by
`fiber`. **D = {i, j, k, l} is four indices over exactly two independent
extents.** `k` is the intermediate vertex of the cocycle triple, drawn
from the same entity set as `i` and `j` — it is not a degree of freedom
that can move on its own.

So `k` cannot drift: it cannot rise unless `dim` rises, and `dim` rises
only through `EntityRegistered`, which is already a receipt. A separate
`k`-deed would mint a degree of freedom the tensor does not have, and a
receipt for a movement that cannot occur is worse than no receipt — it
is a false entry in the ledger.

**The real defect this question surfaces.** D3 states "one index per
deed (the gauge law's shape — declaration == effect, every other index
held still)." That clause is **unsatisfiable for `i`/`j`/`k`**: no deed
can raise `i` while holding `j` and `k` still, because all three read one
bound.

**Strengthened by the §2a ruling, and reframed.** The finding above is
true of the container — `Delta{dim, fiber}` — and the container is not
the capacity (`26_ARTICLES` §5). So the conclusion no longer rests on an
arity argument at all, and holds more simply: **indices are not capacity,
so no index has a receipt to owe.** Capacity is the structure surviving
`Π_G(Δ(V))`; what is receipted is the opening of genuine irreducible
structure, which `GaugeShiftedAt` already carries and `PlaneUnearned`
already guards. The amendment owed to D3 is therefore not "one extent
per deed" but the removal of the index framing from the growth law
entirely — growth is receipted against surviving structure, never
against an index count.
