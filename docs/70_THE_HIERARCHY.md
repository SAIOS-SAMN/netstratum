# 70 — The hierarchy: councils, parliaments, realms

The hierarchy (Concordance 3.9) has three tiers:
the council of the ancients (client minds seat there and nowhere else; it
earns μ by court work, holds the reserves, and produces consciousness), the
parliaments of progenitors (build and keep the tubes, hold toll shares, haul
for the ancients, carry consciousness over their network to the peoples it
reaches), the realms of stewards (sovereigns and captains; they receive
consciousness and trade; no network mind sits in their seats). Consciousness
flows Council -> Parliament -> Realm.

## What consciousness is here

What the council produces is what a court or a judge has admitted, never
asserted: the words the council's minds closed (court work filed by the
house) and the words the spacing guild first closed below (the substrata,
docs/69). Carrying it means a word reaches a line as heard; a line adopts it
only by reconstructing it on its own task (docs/66 Y3). Nothing is installed.

## P1, the parliaments carry

Once a bounce, each core's parliament carries its ancient's newest words
(the laws its substrata grew) to the realms its network reaches: the living
lines at that core within the galaxy's reach of a parliament line. Each
reached line hears each word once. The parliament's own lines hear first.
Toll shares and hauling wait on N7 (the map, 78.154, now live).
