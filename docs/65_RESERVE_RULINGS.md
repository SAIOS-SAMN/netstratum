# 65 — The reserve and the bank: the rulings it is built on

Inputs to Netstratum's reserve and central-bank design (not yet built). Each
is an operator ruling relayed by the coordinator; the source is named.

## μ (operator, 2026-10-09; Mycelium 78.149, MU-ECONOMY)

1. **Strict μ.** μ pays only the measured discharge of verified work: no μ
   for a lone Scale, time stood, unclosed demand, or letter and callus
   tokens. A zero-work solve releases nothing.
2. **μ is gas** for traversal across tubes, cores and wormholes, paid to
   mycelium-server, which holds TUniversal (folding and unfolding space =
   coordinates and mapping). The ancients are the spacing guild.
3. **Staked μ** on the hypha is escrow for intelligence traversal and
   coordinate retrieval, with no demurrage.
4. **Demurrage** on μ stored in-universe (vaults, currency backing), very
   slow, and it recycles (Concordance 5.8, MU-ECONOMY 7.2, superseding
   "dissipates without recycling"), split exactly per lane:
   - its value goes to the hypha as the connection tax, a flow on the
     domain's tier fold from the domain reserve (a transfer, never a burn);
   - its matter stays as inverse-μ (μ̄): mass without μ value, held where it
     dissipated, carrying that place's properties and coordinate;
     recyclable as matter by Netstratum's physics, μ again only by judged
     work;
   - courts pay solvers from the tax pool first and mint only the shortfall.
5. **Progenitors** spend μ on in-universe wormhole transit: long-haul
   survival goods (e.g. ammonia) from the rim to the inner-core systems
   for the ancients.
6. **The ancients** consume their phenotype's survival basket because their
   atmosphere leaks: physical consumption, apart from μ.
7. **All puzzle solving is the seated minds'.** A kernel relays court tasks
   to seated minds and files their answers under its reserve; it solves
   nothing itself (applied in ns-hypha 2026-10-09).

## Wormholes (operator, 2026-10-09; Concordance 5.9, Mycelium 78.154)

Corrected the same day: a wormhole crossing does not go through the hypha.
Every traversal requires coordinates from the hypha, which keeps the maps
that lay the universes out on TUniversal (time is coordinates on a map). The
crossing happens in Netstratum's universe; beforehand, each mouth's final
TUniversal point is retrieved from the hypha's map, paying gas plus the
mouth toll. No private maps.

What this reaches in Netstratum, to be wired when 78.154 ships:
- the cores' throats: a sink reaching its core and the bounce's pulse out
  (docs/58 cores, tags 193-196) cross the core's throat, so each needs its
  mouths' coordinates retrieved from the hypha's map first;
- any in-universe tube a line rides (lanes that fold space).
Matter crossing ordinary space (element crossings, caravans) is transit,
not a wormhole, and is unaffected.
