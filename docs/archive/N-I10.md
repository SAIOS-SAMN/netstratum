## 3 · Why: a minted word is one line's until it is heard

Consilience (N-I8) mints a word — but it sits in the sun as the line's own.
Nothing yet carries it into a second vessel's perspective. Hearing is that
carry: the speaker's word, offered under a frame, is reduced by the hearer
against its own basin. If it drains, the two perspectives already agreed
(the word was understood); if it walls, the hearer learns it — adopts the
crystal onto `grown[B]`, so its next think seeds from a word it did not
invent. That adopt, from B's own reduce and not a copy, is how a word
spreads between minds — the first motion of a shared culture, still exact,
still `reduce_pair`, still no chat.

## 4 · The utterance and the hear

```
utterance  = a folded word from the sun (a MintedWord: orbit + expansion),
             re-formed under a frame (the hearer's gauge, or a rotation —
             "another frame")
hear(B)    = observe(word_as_measurement, grown[B])          // B's own reduce
   Accounted → HEARD: B's basin already drains it. No adopt, nothing minted.
   Minted    → LEARNED: B walls it and `adopt`s the crystal onto grown[B] ONLY.
   (Carried) → the residual is B's next observation, same law as think.
```

- **The word is offered as a measurement**, not handed as a crystal: apply
  the word's expansion to a probe → the pair `(probe, word(probe))` encodes
  the word; B peels and reduces it. B reaching the same orbit is B hearing;
  B walling is B not-yet-knowing.
- **Another frame.** The utterance may be re-formed under a different gauge
  (a rotation/relabel of the word). B drains it iff its basin accounts the
  frame-shift — a word heard through a different perspective and still
  understood is the strong signal; a wall is honest non-understanding.
- **Adopt is B-only.** A walled word lands in `grown[B]` through `adopt`
  (idempotent, N-I2). `grown[A]` is never read to fill `grown[B]`.

---

## 5 · The cost

- **Hearing by think costs.** When B reduces the offered word (a fresh peel
  + reduce), it pays `observe_price` from the orb leisure bank (N-I7),
  exactly as a look does. A line that cannot pay does not hear this beat —
  it fasts. Hearing is looking.
- **`pre_reach` stays free.** Reading the sun (folded words as letters) in
  the ordinary reduce arm is uncharged — the free half of the mind. Only the
  fresh reduce of an offered word bills.
- No `mint_cost` on a heard word: hearing adopts a crystal (learning), it
  does not mint value or a new vocabulary entry.
