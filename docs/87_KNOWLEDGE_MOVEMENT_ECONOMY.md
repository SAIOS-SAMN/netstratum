# 87 — Knowledge, movement and the economy: one physics

## The principle

Physics sets what is there, how it turns, and what crossing it costs.
Knowledge sets how much of it a people can reach. Every exchange is a
carriage along a trajectory; the economy is minds trading what their
knowledge let them reach and carry. Nothing is a cell, a tile or a table
row.

**One grammar: knowledge adds halvings to a carriage against a physical
bound.**

## 1. Knowledge

Rungs and words, earned by thinking (the ladder, the forge) and traded (the
knowledge market). Its worth is never declared: it is what it opens below.

## 2. Land

- Wild: `⌊500 · T · (1 − 2^(−⌊h⌋)) / T_home⌋` (docs/86).
- Cultivated: `1 − 2^(−⌊h + k⌋)`, k the people's knowledge in halvings;
  it approaches the full flux T and never passes it. Past it a people
  reaches more flux: more worlds, or a finer lock with its star (Ψ_w,
  docs/82).
- A people stands on a CAP of its world's geoid, where its medium takes up
  most flux; regions are caps, neighbours are caps that overlap (the
  non-Euclidean Venn). Replaces the 64-tile board, the zone table and the
  square surface.

## 3. Movement

- **Place:** a point in degrees on a body's own geoid, its equipotential,
  set by its holding, its core share κ and its spin (docs/88).
- **A path is a turning:** an arc is a rotor in degrees (docs/85,
  `forms::frame`). A trajectory is the composition of rotors through each
  tier's map, read in the observer's frame (A6), each frame its closed form
  `φ + ω·t` (law 5).
- **Between bodies:** a chain of arcs on nested geoids joined at throats
  (`enclosure::route`, the arcs added).
- **Cost of a carriage:** the pull crossed at each throat `H/(R + H)`; the
  arc on each sphere; the SENSE (prograde rides the frame's turn, the lever;
  retrograde pairs with it and pays the paired share, law 6); the WINDOW
  (both ends turn, so a crossing exists where their phases meet, from the
  `p/q` lock of their periods, `convergents`).
- **Knowledge:** knowing a frame's turning lets a carrier ride prograde and
  find windows. Roads, gates and wormholes are knowledge made physical: they
  hold a window open on a link, or cut its pull.

## 4. Hulls

Shaped matter whose reach is how deep a throat it can climb out of, from
the people's knowledge of holding. No shipyard tile. Captains are the lines
whose hulls ride the windows.

## 5. Matter

Goods are the world's own era-1 composition, flowing. A kind is workable
when the people's chemistry knows how it closes; assay is knowing a kind.

## 6. Markets and money

- A hub is where carriages meet: caps that overlap at an open window.
- A good's value depends on where and when it can arrive.
- Prices are cleared ratios, moneyness is measured, STAMP retires
  (docs/74). Tolls on mass and information carried (Concordance 5.4), at
  the pull and sense of the link used.

## 7. The tiers

Ancients pure knowledge; progenitors doorways (windows held open); stewards
the realms passage over them forms; sovereigns their own system's flux;
captains carriage; the rest their caps (`tiers`).

## Design laws

Arcs, rotors and shares only (no squares, no norms); no grids, no fill
orders; halvings whole (ℤ), shares and phases ℚ; no habitable zone; each
body by its own gravity and density; both senses, pairing closes.
