# 81 — Convergence and collision: the law of playing with time

Operator (2026-10-13): TUniversal is offered as a quantum state; a universe
chains its own ledger to it, perpendicular (orthogonal), as a local quantum
state. In a universe of potentials and of time as coordinates, where
timelines meet there are two outcomes, CONVERGENCE and COLLISION. The
ledger pays locally for convergence and charges for collision. "You can play
with time, but if you cause a collision you get a ticket, paid dearly in
your citizens and your local stewards." Stewards keep local time;
sectors with too many collisions are shut down. Advanced civilizations
seek continuous convergence, or large convergences with minor collisions.

Status: scoped 2026-10-13; not built. It builds on docs/80 (time and the
observers), docs/79 (quantum), docs/75 (realms), docs/74 (the hierarchy).

## The structure

- **TUniversal** is the shared quantum state, one per fleet: the hypha's
  clock.
- **A universe's ledger** is chained orthogonally to it. By mycelium's ruling
  78.152d each LEDGER entry already pairs the universe's cosmic time `u` with
  TUniversal's point (docs/80).
- **Each observer's proper time** is a further local axis inside the
  universe (docs/80).
- **Where two timelines' records MEET,** there are two outcomes:
  - **CONVERGENCE:** the records agree. The same event, the same causal
    order, translations to the same cosmic time.
  - **COLLISION:** the records contradict. For example:
    - a crossing that arrives before it left;
    - two observers placing causally linked events in opposite order;
    - two claims on the same mass at the same cosmic time.

## Where collisions can happen (the physics that lets the law fire)

In a deterministic universe, collisions arise only where agents act on time.
Without these, the universe is always convergent:

1. **Wormhole mouths out of synchronisation.** The progenitors keep mouths
   on the ancients' clock (docs/80); a mouth that drifts sends crossings out
   of order.
2. **Conflicting translations.** Stewards translate members' proper times
   onto cosmic time; two translations that disagree about a causal pair
   collide.
3. **Contested mass at one cosmic time.** Two claims on the same mass at the
   same moment. This needs shared space (docs/77, items 1 and 3).

## The ledger's law

| outcome | what it does | read from |
|---|---|---|
| convergence | pays locally: the realm's ledger is credited, as netstratum's consilience law rewards two vantages agreeing (two-sided receipts are convergence in miniature) | the annals: events recorded by two observers that agree |
| collision | a TICKET, physical, never a fine: two incompatible records cannot both persist, so the annals keep one, and the other's matter, population or passage is lost at the collision site | the annals: the event kept, and what was lost |

- **"Paid in citizens" is the physics.** It is the destructive interference
  of incompatible records, not a penalty table.
- **"Paid by the stewards"** is their standing: the progenitors promote and
  replace stewards by the realm's measured outcomes (docs/75), and
  collisions are among them.
- **The parallel at the hypha** is slashing: a fold not yet final is
  slashable, and a bond is lost on a bad fold. Here the bond is local, the
  realm's time-keeping.

## Duties by tier

| tier | duty in time |
|---|---|
| ANCIENTS | hold the reference clock (cosmic time at the cores); assign the progenitors |
| PROGENITORS | keep their wormhole mouths synchronised to the ancients' clock; close their mouths toward a sector that collides too often |
| STEWARDS | keep the realm's local time-keeping events: place members' events on cosmic time, settle order (causal first, then cosmic time), and answer for the realm's collisions |
| SOVEREIGNS, CAPTAINS | act on proper time; their acts are the events that converge or collide |

## Shutdown

- A sector (a realm) is SHUT DOWN when its collisions per span of cosmic time
  exceed what its network can carry: the progenitors close their mouths
  toward it, and its systems fall back on their own science and hulls, as
  every system without a wormhole lives.
- **The threshold is a derivation, OPEN.** It is read from the annals and
  derived from the network's capacity (its mouths, their throughput, the
  realm's span), never a dial.

## What emerges

Selection: realms with good time-keeping keep their networks and grow, and
colliding realms are cut off. Advanced civilizations learn, by DECIDE
(docs/73), to produce continuous convergence, or large convergences with
minor collisions. Nothing in it is scripted.

## Usurpation: replacing a progenitor lineage with a non-progenitor one

Lineage in netstratum is by the first parent (`ancients::root_of`), not
gendered. That first-parent rule is itself an authored placeholder.

If locals put a sovereign, non-progenitor lineage in a progenitor lineage's
place, the physics answers, with no rule against it:

1. **The network refuses it.** No hyperspace traversal without a progenitor
   assigned by the ancients (docs/74). The usurper holds the gates without an
   assignment, so passage is refused and the network goes dark in that
   sector.
2. **Time decoheres.** The usurper has no link to the ancients' clock, so its
   mouths drift. Any crossing forced through arrives out of order: a
   collision, its ticket paid in the sector's citizens and passage.
3. **The realm dissolves.** A realm exists only where a progenitor network
   carries passage (docs/75), and its steward was promoted by the displaced
   progenitors. The realm shrinks back to systems on their own science.
4. **Shutdown follows repeated collisions.** The surrounding progenitors close
   their mouths toward the sector to protect their own timelines.

**The legitimate path stays open.**
- The throat's contest (G3) is open to any lineage that out-holds the keeper.
- A master's envoys can keep a throat for a core period and ascend by deed
  (docs/72).
- An ancient lineage bears progenitors of its own, assigned and
  synchronised.

Authority comes from assignment and synchronisation, never from holding the
gates.

## Collision-born, and reconciliation

- **Nothing is "supposed to" happen.** The annals record what happened, never
  what should have; a "supposed" timeline would be a script (docs/76).
- **COLLISION-BORN.** A birth whose causal past runs through an UNRESOLVED
  collision: the parents' meeting, or the conditions of the birth, came
  through a crossing the network never synchronised. It is read from the
  annals, never declared. It is the physical form of "children who were not
  supposed to happen".
- **They cannot converge.** Every record of a collision-born line carries the
  unresolved collision in its causal past, so each meeting with the main
  timeline (a trade, a crossing, a claim on mass) is a further collision.
  - **They carry the tickets.** The losses at those meetings fall where the
    incompatible records sit: their own population, matter and passage. Not
    a rule, but because their existence is the unresolved contradiction.
  - **Their sector decays toward shutdown:** its collision count rises, the
    progenitors close their mouths, and they live cut off on their own
    science.
- **Reconciliation, forward only.** When a collision-born line's sector is
  re-synchronised (a progenitor assigned by the ancients brings its mouths
  back onto the ancients' clock), its later records converge with the main
  timeline. The unresolved collision stays in its past; from that point its
  meetings converge. The annals keep the whole: born of a collision, later
  reconciled. Nothing is ever erased: backward erasure would rewrite the
  append-only annals and break causal order (docs/79, docs/80).

## Branches, attraction and pruning

### Branches

- When a collision cannot be resolved by the loss of one record, the
  timeline may BRANCH: the main timeline continues on the ancients' clock,
  and a branch carries the incompatible records.
- **The split is an event**, at a point on the map, recorded in both annals,
  each append-only.
- **Open:** whether a branch is a new universe (a new origin, which by
  mycelium's law is a new domain) or a sub-history within this universe's
  domain, held by its annals. Netstratum's proposal is the latter, so
  branches stay inside the universe and can come back. To be ruled with
  mycelium, since a branch that leaves its universe would need its own
  origin.

### DIVERGENCE

The separation, on the map, between a branch's records and the main
timeline's: the interval between corresponding events (the same subject's
records on each side). Time is a coordinate (docs/80), so divergence is a
distance, measured exactly as era 1 measures distances (the squared chord,
here over the time axis and place).

### Temporal attraction: gravity along the time axis

- Branches are pulled back toward the main timeline by the same law as
  masses in era 1's geometry (xylarium §10.3, docs/78), the flux through the
  cone joining them:

      g(D) = G_k W / (|S^k| D^k)

  - `W`: the main timeline's WEIGHT, the records that converge on it (mass,
    population and passage carried, read from the annals);
  - `D`: the branch's divergence;
  - `k`: the cone's dimension at that divergence, from the bounce's torsion
    as in space (k = 2 close, toward 1 across many pitches).
- **Close branches are pulled hard; far ones barely at all.** No new force:
  time's attraction is gravity along the time axis of the map.

### Temporal anchors (chronometry)

- **ANCHORS** pin a timeline to the clock:
  - the LEDGER entries' crossing pairs (cosmic time `u` against the
    TUniversal point, 78.152d), where the universe's timeline is pinned to
    the hypha's;
  - the ancients' clock at the cores (each core's bell);
  - the progenitors' synchronised wormhole mouths, each one an anchor on the
    network.
- **A branch converges back only onto an anchored timeline.** The denser the
  anchors, the tighter the pull: anchoring raises the main timeline's
  effective weight `W` near its anchors.
- **Duties:** the ancients keep the clock; the progenitors keep their mouths
  anchored; the stewards keep the realm's time-keeping events (its local
  anchors).

### SUPERFLUIDITY

- Timelines that stay close and converge continuously are COHERENT in phase.
  Passage among them flows without friction: the melt on the network falls
  toward zero in a coherent realm.
- **Divergence is friction:** passage and trade cost more as timelines
  diverge.
- **The law** (to derive exactly in era 1): a route's melt is its
  geometric melt (distance, technology, hulls) scaled down by the coherence
  of the timelines it joins, reaching zero for a perfectly coherent pair.
- **So convergence pays automatically.** Coherent realms move goods almost
  freely; divergent ones bleed melt. This is the economic force behind
  "advanced civilizations seek convergence".

### The point of no return: TERMINAL NODES

- A branch ESCAPES when it diverges faster than the main timeline's pull can
  bring it back: the escape condition of the cone law (its rate of
  divergence at `D` exceeds the escape rate `sqrt(2 · ∫ g dD)`, kept exact by
  comparing squares). Derived, not a dial.
- An escaped branch is a TERMINAL NODE: it has no path back to the forward
  positions.
- **The danger is weight.** A terminal node carrying great weight
  (population, mass, passage) would cause a great collision if it were ever
  forced back. The heavier and farther it is, the worse.

### PRUNING: the progenitors' automated systems

- **The progenitors learn a `prune` decision** (DECIDE, docs/73):
  - rows: the branches attached to their network;
  - features: divergence `D`, weight, divergence rate, whether escaped;
  - loss: the collision cost when an unpruned branch is reconverged, against
    the convergence lost when a recoverable branch is pruned.
- **Pruning closes a terminal branch's links to the network.** Its mouths
  close, it is unanchored, and it no longer attracts or collides. It is NOT
  erased: the branch's annals persist, append-only, and it lives on its own.
- **The main timeline stays light and coherent:** branches at the edge are
  pruned before they carry too much weight; branches within reach are left
  for attraction to bring home.

## What it pays in: ψ, COHERENCE (local physics, never μ)

**Unlinked from μ.** Transport and transit inside a universe are NOT paid in
μ. μ is the hypha's currency, for crossings between universes and the
court's work (Concordance 5.8, boundary 1). Inside the universe, transit is
paid in a quantity of the universe's own physics, produced and spent by its
local economies. This is the "economy produced by the progenitors" that local
wormhole traversal is paid in (operator, 2026-10-13).

**The quantity: ψ, coherence.** ψ is the physicist's symbol for a quantum
state, and for a superfluid's order parameter, the very coherence that makes
flow frictionless.

**The alternatives weighed:**

| symbol | meaning it would carry | in use today |
|---|---|---|
| **ψ (recommended)** | coherence: the quantum state's order, superfluidity's order parameter | an orb's inclination `ColonyOrb.psi`, the flat orbit layer that era 1 retires (orbs become S^60 bodies), and the thermal model; free in era 1 |
| κ | curvature, or conductance | netstratum's morphogen; mycelium's compartment round `κ` (the sensor layer) |
| σ | conductivity, which a superfluid makes infinite | the minds' instinct stress (σ = 0 sated); thermal |

Until era 1 retires the orb's ψ, the code names it `coherence` and the
documents write ψ.

**How ψ arises and is lost (exact, read from the annals):**
- **Minted by convergence.** When two records meet and agree, ψ is produced
  equal to the weight that converged (the mass, population or passage the
  agreeing records carry), credited to the realm whose time-keeping
  produced it (its steward's account).
- **Destroyed by collision.** At a collision, the ψ equal to the weight lost
  is destroyed with it. The realm's ψ falls by its collisions.
- **No other source.** ψ is never minted by decree, never converted from μ,
  STAMP or any civilization's money, and never created except by convergence.

**What ψ does:**
- **It pays for transit.** Passage over a progenitor's wormhole costs ψ,
  paid to the progenitors who keep it anchored; the toll is the coherence
  the crossing draws on the network.
- **It is superfluidity.** A realm's ψ against its passage is its coherence:
  the factor that brings its routes' melt toward zero.
- **It is earned, not issued.** A coherent realm earns ψ and travels freely;
  a colliding realm loses ψ, cannot pay for transit, and is cut off, before
  any shutdown. The progenitors earn ψ by keeping coherent networks.

**Against docs/74 (the emergent economy):**
- ψ is not a civilization's money: it is physics, held by realms and
  progenitors, and it pays for transit alone.
- Civilizations' emergent moneys still pay for goods.
- A civilization obtains transit by trading its goods or money for ψ with
  those who hold it, at cleared ratios: the progenitors' economy.
