## 3. The creed manifold

A **creed** is a derived position on the belief manifold — not a fourth
axis (the three axes stand structurally unchanged, 12 §9) and not
stored. It reads from the line's chronicle — its friction receipts,
compassion and breach books, crystallized doctrine nodes, and gauge
walks — exactly the way function and tier read today.

- **The creed space is the gauge-key manifold.** A line's graded key
  (GAUGE-N) plus its cohesion books locate its creed; two creeds'
  distance is the wrapped key gap (`key_distance`).
- **Schism = divergence** (tag 43, landed): a creed under sustained
  internal friction cleaves — the branches take opposite phases (±Δ).
  "Hive Mind Splinters" is not a table entry; it is the landed cleave
  arriving under the friction reading.
- **Reconciliation = confluence** (tag 44) priced by **BRIDGE-1**
  (11 §3.1): the key gap prices the venting, the resonant remainder
  encases as the reconciled line's opening bank. **LINEAGE-2** (landed)
  carries the provenance: a reconciled creed records both predecessor creeds,
  and `resolve_heads` reads every root.
- **The friction gate** is ELEVATE-1's gate 2 read through the creed:
  sustained coherence with the line's own friction receipts settled —
  measured, never a timer. Descent is the retirement region, reachable
  from creed space and escapable from it.
