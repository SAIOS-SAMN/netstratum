# 126 — The vacuum as a Möbius strip: the core's pressure on the twist

This document derives the continuum and the vacuum as one Möbius strip, the
two faces of one surface carrying one pressure. The force that exudes from
the core is the pressure of the quantum torsion's twist. On the continuum
face it releases matter. On the vacuum face, the same strip read past its
half twist, it consumes.

## 1. What was not at its base

- **The pass jumps a flat ½ on every spatial plane.** On a round orientation
  three half turns compose to the identity, so the pass carries no twist at
  all. This is the flat torus's antipode (docs/125 §3.3, #158).
- **The pressure exudes on one face only.** The release takes the twist's
  pressure. The vacuum's consumption at the throat, recycling's deconstructed
  share `e = E/(S + E)`, reads none of it. The same twist is felt on one face
  of the strip and not on the other.

## 2. The pass is the strip's half twist

A Möbius strip is one surface with a half twist. Going round it once turns
the face over; going round twice returns it. The continuum and the vacuum are
its two faces, and the ring (`δ = ½`) is its centre line. The half twist is
not a jump applied on every plane alike. It is the twist itself, the core's
winding locked into the mote's lead:

    M: (δ, ψ_k, s) ↦ (1 − δ, ψ_k + ½·t_k, −s)        Σ_k ½·t_k = ½

- **One pass** turns the face over (`δ ↦ 1 − δ`), reverses the helix
  (`s ↦ −s`, the torsion's sign with it), and turns each plane by its share of
  the half twist. On the share circle the whole pass is half a turn; on the
  vector it is the quarter turn of #183 (`K_ψ = 2·K_vector`, #241 §3).
- **Two passes** return the face, the sense and every share. The phases have
  advanced one whole twist, `ψ_k + t_k`, summing to one turn. The winding is
  kept, never erased, so `M∘M` is the identity on what the strip carries and
  a whole turn on the twist. The joint ruling (#241) holds the inversion "as
  turns with the winding kept, not a substitution".
- **Untilted** (no lead), the half twist is shared evenly, a sixth of a turn
  a plane.

## 3. One pressure, two faces

The force that exudes from the core is the twist's pressure, `P_k` on each
plane (§0). The strip carries it on both faces:

- **On the continuum face it releases.** What leaves the throat is the
  release share composed with the plane pressures, `s′ = s ⊕ P₁ ⊕ P₂ ⊕ P₃`
  (built).
- **On the vacuum face it consumes.** Past the half twist the helix is
  reversed, so the same pressures read with their sense flipped. What the
  throat deconstructs of the inbound matter is the deconstructed share
  composed with the reversed pressures:

      e′ = e ⊕ (−P₁) ⊕ (−P₂) ⊕ (−P₃)

  `−P` is the flow law's inverse. Inverted on every facet, the vacuum's
  pressure is the continuum's, read on its other face.
- **The two faces cancel exactly:** `(x ⊕ ΣP) ⊕ (−ΣP) = x` for any share
  `x` (the flow law is a group). A strip with no twist (`P = 0`) leaves both
  shares as they were.
- **Where the twist winds against the helix** (a plane pressure negative),
  the vacuum consumes more than its bare share and the continuum releases
  less. Where it winds with it, the reverse. "Equal or greater pressure to
  consume" is the vacuum face read where the twist's sense favours it.

## 3a. Heat on the strip

**The operator asked (2026-11-11):** "are you accounting for the inversion to
cold via your heat derivation".

- **Heat crosses the pass unchanged.** Heat is motion out of step, the spread

  `I` leaves every reading that names no face unchanged (docs/104 §0: turning
  on its own leg, phases, in-step). The face enters only where heat is made

  counterweight's transfer between bodies (docs/104 §2, "cooling here is
  heating there"). The inversion to cold is a transfer between bodies on the
  two faces, never a relabelling of one body's heat.
- **Heat travels with the matter that carries it, mixed by holding.** The
  first read of §2 showed heat unaccounted at bell 558 growing from
  −[2;08) to −[11;20)·60⁻². That was heat leaving with matter unbooked:
  - a star's outburst, its shed layers, its dissolutions and its infall made
    motes with heat 0;

  - motes falling into a star dropped theirs.

  Each now carries its source's heat per holding: the star's
  `agitation_of(reservoir) + heat` (`formation::star_heat`), a system's

- **Superseded as the method by docs/127** (operator 2026-11-11): moving heat

- **Still open:** the counterweight's pull channel (docs/104 §2) against
