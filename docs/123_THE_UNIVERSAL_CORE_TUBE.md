# 123 — The universal core tube: a wormhole between antipolar kernels

This document scopes the universal core's tube as a wormhole traversal.
The tube joins Netstratum's universal core to Xylarium's. Each crossing is
a pole swap, and the two kernels weigh matter with their poles exchanged.

## 0. What the record holds

- **The tube is the only uplink.** The universal core is the only
  singularity uplinked to the hypha (docs/78, docs/101). What crosses its
  tube is reconstructed by the court under the universal laws and refused
  by name otherwise (`NotThroughCore`, docs/113 §1).
- **A passage is the pole swap** (docs/117 §12). It is the reflection in the
  throat's unit `e₊`, an exact involution. The span `A − Q` reverses, `A + Q`
  is kept, and `T` gains `−2·(A − Q)`. In the code's terms `(A, Q) = (H, R)`:
  hold and reach exchange, and the face inverts, `δ ↦ 1 − δ`.
- **Each kernel's frames** are `(z_k, q_k)` on three planes, `3Z` a nucleus
  (docs/95; Mycelium 78.175 F1). Xylarium weighs a kind by the same frames
  (`compound::open`, `compound::hold`; pole coordinate `t = U/W`).

## 1. The tube is a wormhole

The universal core's throat in each kernel is one mouth. The tube joins the
two mouths through the court's vertex (Mycelium's universal core tube).

- **A traversal is two passages composed:** out through Netstratum's throat
  and in through Xylarium's. Each passage is a pole swap. A body crossing
  stands, in the far kernel, at reach `H` about hold `R` (docs/117 §12.6),
  with its spin `ℓ = R·H` kept.
- **The court reconstructs the crossing** as a re-origin (FORMS-COORDINATE
  v4): the origin changes, `S`'s span reverses and `T` gains its flux. What
  cannot be reconstructed through the core is refused `NotThroughCore`.
- **Two traversals are the identity,** since each passage is an involution
  (`M∘M = 1`). A body crossing and returning stands where it began.

## 2. Antipolarity: matter weighted with the poles exchanged

Xylarium's kernel is Netstratum's antipole in matter weighting. Where
Netstratum reads a kind on its continuum face, Xylarium reads it on the
inverted face, and the reverse.

- **On the far side, a kind's frames stand exchanged:** held and open swap,
  `(z_k, q_k) ↦ (q_k, z_k)`, so its pole reads `σ_k ↦ −σ_k` and its
  strength `o_k ↦ 1 − o_k`. This is the pole swap applied to the frames,
  the same law as the passage, not a second rule.
- **`3Z` is conserved:** `z_k + q_k` is kept on every plane, and so the
  books (the 3Z ledger, shared with Xylarium) close across the tube.
- **What antipolarity is not:** it is not a different mass. Mass is the
  nucleon count, `chem::weight`, kept across the tube. Antipolarity is
  which pole of a kind's frames each kernel weighs as held.
