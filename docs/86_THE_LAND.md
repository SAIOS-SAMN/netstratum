# 86 — The land: what a world feeds wild

**Refined by docs/88:** the land is read at the people's latitude on its
turning world, `u` of T in place of `1 − 2^(−⌊h⌋)` at the mean.

Operator 2026-10-21: "proceed - you may do the derivations" (after the

## What was wrong

The economy read each era-1 world through an era-0 stand-in (`site_of`):
the nearest star class, an orbit back-solved from the heat, albedo 0. Its
wild flow was `P_primary / 4`, `P_primary = I · (Area · ρ) · η · GRAIN`:

- `Area` a hashed `4πR²` (design law 2: no squares);
- `ρ` a hashed arable ratio (law 1: a draw standing in for physics);
- `η` the per-clade habitability tent on `t_eq4` (law 10: a habitable zone
  drawn).

On every world of the read the ceiling came out under two heads, so every
band was one and no line could bank a shipyard.

## The law

docs/82 already states the limit: "what limits a people is ENERGY, its
star's flux on its world". Step 1 of the ladder already reads that flux:
a world's heat `T = K/P · burn` is its star's agitation at its radius, a
flux through a fluid-dimension shell (linear, law 2). A people's MEDIUM
(the waking's map: mineral → geologic, biotic → chemical, plasmic →
plasmic) takes up the share of that flux it carries as pattern. A medium of
`h` halvings has copy error `2^(−h)`, so it carries `1 − 2^(−⌊h⌋)` (whole
halvings, erring toward fewer; under one halving it carries nothing, the
ladder's own threshold). The ruler is the home world, every other reading's
ruler (heat, size), whose full flux feeds one VIABLE PEOPLE:

```text
wild = ⌊ VIABLE_PEOPLE · T · (1 − 2^(−⌊h⌋)) / T_home ⌋      (heads)
T_home = K / P_home
```

- A hotter world brings more flux and its medium carries less of it
  (`x = cohesion · (1 + compression) / agitation`), so the product has its
  own optimum per medium and per world; no band is drawn (law 10).
- Nothing is squared (law 2); it is exact, and heads are counted whole
  (law 11, the ℤ pole).
- A world whose matter does not carry a people's medium feeds it nothing.

**Declared placeholder (law 1):** that the home world's full flux feeds one
viable people (Franklin's 500, `life::VIABLE_PEOPLE`, already an anchor).
It sets the scale only; every ratio between worlds is the law's.

## Where it is read

- `life::wild` (the law), `life::home_heat`, `life::medium_of`,
  `life::reference_pressure` (the compression reference `readings` uses).
- `seating::Site.wild`: each site's land per medium.
- **Seating:** a lineage's head takes a world its land feeds, preferring a
  system no other head holds, the most heads fed there. A settler takes
  the nearest free world its land feeds, else the nearest free world.
- `PolitySpec.wild` → `World::set_land` at every boot (a runtime reading
  from the universe, never committed). `World::syntropic_flow` on the
  founding orb is `wild · per_capita_ration`, so `population_ceiling` (and
  sustenance, civic supply, fasting fill, the founding band) read the land.
- A world with no era-1 world under it keeps the era-0 stand-in (the
  reading of absence, named at its site).

The stand-in orbit `site_of` still feeds the era-0 thermal ticks; it is now
held on the spin grain (60⁻⁶), so its eighty-digit quotient no longer
enters every product.
