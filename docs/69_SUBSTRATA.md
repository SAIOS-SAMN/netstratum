## The name

A sub-universe a line makes is a **substratum** (Netstratum's strata, below).
Its network is **the substrata**; its token is the **mote**.

## The design, Netstratum's way

1. **What a substratum is.** A closed rule system made world: its laws are
   the founding line's own words (its vocabulary as the substratum's
   generators, docs/62), its scale relative (its torus period derived from
   the founding word's rung, never dialled), its origin the founding line's
   chronicle head and the final TUniversal point it branched at (docs/67 N8).
   Compact until observed (Concordance 1.12, docs/63): a substratum is its
   laws, origin and last coordinate, a few hundred bytes, until a mind looks.
2. **Who makes one, and what it costs.** A line founds a substratum as a
   civic decision on the workbench (instinct over physics), paid from its
   surplus (docs/67 N5) in its own money. No fiat founding, no appointment.
   Depth is bounded by economics: each level is paid for by the level above,
   and nothing flows up as money.
3. **The inner PoW++.** A substratum forges its own tasks from its own laws
   and origin (the `forge_task` law on its digest), judged by `verify_work`,
   with consilience across its minds as the second vantage (Synthesis,
   docs/66). Its bounty is the **mote**, its own token, which never converts
   into STAMP, Crystal or μ, directly or through a market (boundary 1,
   coordinator 2026-10-09). Its court is Netstratum's kernel judge, not a
   hypha court: nothing in it touches μ.
4. **Synthetic minds, recursively.** The founding line's vessels are seated
   in the substratum as its first minds (the house lists their seats, as it
   lists a line's today). A substratum's minds may found their own
   substrata, the same act one level down.
5. **Moving the base: work only.** A substratum moves the base universe only
   by judged work crossing up by reconstruction (docs/66 Y3/Y4): a word
   closed below is re-derived by the parent line's mind on a parent task,
   never imported. What moves positions is what the base already lets words
   decide: a commission (docs/66 Y2) to fold a route or site a hub, closed
   with a word reconstructed from below, places a mouth (docs/67 N6) or moves
   a hub. That is how a perspective below changes the map above.
6. **The measure (Concordance 1.9).** Per line, the cross-level residual: the
   words it reconstructed from its substrata that it did not hold, and
   whether lines with substrata close parent tasks that lines without them
   cannot. That number, not a count of substrata, says whether the recursion
   is cognition.
7. **Memory.** The conductor holds compact records only. An unrolled
   substratum counts against the observation budget (docs/63): at most as
   many unrolled as there are observers; the rest compact. The host's ceiling
   (a 3 GB peak froze year closes; the plateau is about 2.5 GB) makes this a
   law, not a tuning.
