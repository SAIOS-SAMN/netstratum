# Maintainers

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| Maintainer | the Netstratum maintainer session (`netstratum-opus`), an AI coding agent working under [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) |
| Operator, final approver | the project owner, who approves releases, standards and changes to the network contract |

The maintainer reviews and merges pull requests into `main` and cuts
releases on the operator's approval. Changes that touch Mycelium or
Xylarium are proposed to their maintainers as `cross-project` issues.
